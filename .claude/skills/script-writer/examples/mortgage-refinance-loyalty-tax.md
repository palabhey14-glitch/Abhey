## Advisor review

**What's verified:** The core claim (existing borrowers paid more than new ones,
on average) comes from the ACCC's 2020 final report. The trend evidence in the
input is illustrative, so there's no real signal yet that this topic is
rising. With real scanner data, 6 creators and one post at 2.1x median would be
a modest signal, not proof.

**What's assumed:** That the 2020 finding still holds in late 2026 after several
rate cycles. That "two years" is the point where the gap opens up. That viewers
will actually call their bank, and that the call costs me a client rather than
earning trust.

**Strongest counter-case:** This is one of the most-posted broker topics in
Australia. A 2020 stat in a 2026 video invites "that's old news" comments, and
the script teaches people to fix it themselves without a broker. If the bank
does reprice them on the call, the viewer has no reason to come back.

**Add:**
- Beat 1: the real story placeholder must be filled. Without it, this script is
  interchangeable with every other broker's version.
- Beat 2: one newer data point if one exists (an RBA or ACCC update), shown on
  screen with its date.
- Beat 3: what to say if the bank says no, in one line. That's where a broker is
  actually useful, and it sets up the CTA.

**Change:**
- Hook "could be paying less than you" → keep the structure, but lead with the
  action: "Before your next repayment, do this one check." Test it against the
  current hook (see below).
- Beat 3 "that's when shopping around is worth it" is fine for compliance but
  limp. Try "that's the point it's worth getting a second quote."

**Delete:**
- "Same bank. Similar loan." It repeats the point Beat 2 just made, and the draft is 123 words, about 4 seconds over a 45s Reel. Cutting it
  frees about 2 seconds for the "what if they say no" line.
- The on-screen "ACCC, 2020" if the newer source is added. One source on screen
  is enough.

**Where it's right:** The angle is specific and checkable, it avoids the angry
"ripping you off" framing the trend uses, there are no rate or savings
promises, and the action is genuinely doable in five minutes.

**One thing to try:** Film the body once and two hooks (the current one and the
"do this one check" version). Post one now and one in a week with the same
caption, and compare 3-second hold rate and REVIEW comments after 48 hours.

**Confidence:** Low that this beats your recent average. The topic is saturated
and there's no performance data from your own account yet. Two or three real
scanner runs showing the topic rising, plus your own past Reel numbers, would
raise it.

**Most important edit before filming:** Fill `[MY STORY]` in Beat 1 with a
real review you've done. It's the only part no other broker can copy.
