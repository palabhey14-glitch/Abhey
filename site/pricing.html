Start Entry Level Coaching
What’s included in Entry Level Guidance?

Entry Level Guidance includes a customised meal plan or macro plan, a personalised meal plan ebook, a structured workout program, check-ins every two weeks, plan updates every two weeks, coaching communication by email, and access to the Custom Rewired Coaching App for daily logging, progress tracking, habit tracking and your plans.

How much does Entry Level Guidance cost?

Entry Level Guidance is AUD $80 per week, billed weekly. It’s an ongoing subscription that continues until you cancel, and your first four weeks are a committed coaching period.

Where do I find my meal plan and workout program?

On Entry Level Guidance, your meal or macro plan and your workout program are delivered through the Custom Rewired Coaching App. Once I’ve set up your account, you sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, on your phone or your computer.

How often are check-ins?

On Entry Level Guidance, check-ins are completed every two weeks.

How often is my plan updated?

On Entry Level Guidance, I review and update your plan every two weeks.

How do I talk to my coach?

On Entry Level Guidance, coaching replies and communication happen by email. Your plans, logging and tracking stay in the app. WhatsApp support is part of Premium Coaching.

When can I cancel?

Your first four weeks of Entry Level Guidance are a committed coaching period. Once your fourth weekly payment has been taken, you can cancel your subscription at any time by emailing coaching@abheyrewired.com. Cancelling stops future payments, and your coaching continues until the end of the week you’ve already paid for.

How does the eight-week satisfaction policy work?

If you’re not satisfied with your results after completing eight weeks of Entry Level Guidance, I’ll refund the coaching fees you paid for the previous four weeks, which is your last four weekly payments (AUD $320). It covers those four weeks, not the full eight, and it applies to Entry Level Guidance only.

How is Entry Level Guidance different from Premium Coaching?

Premium Coaching is AUD $2,249.99 for three months, one to one. It adds weekly training updates, fortnightly video check-ins with a weekly voice note, my personal WhatsApp 7 days a week with replies within 12 hours, a plan to keep your results after week 12, and the Week 8 Progress Promise.

Entry Level Guidance is AUD $80 per week, with check-ins and plan updates every two weeks and coaching by email. The comparison above sets them side by side.
