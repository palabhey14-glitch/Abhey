Key points

  • We collect what we need to assess your application and coach you. Some of it is health information, which the law treats as sensitive.
  • We ask for detailed health information after you join, in the app, with a separate consent choice. You don’t have to give health details to apply.
  • Only you and Abhey can see your app records and progress photos. Photos are kept in private storage and are never public.
  • The app’s database and photo storage are hosted in Sydney, Australia. Some other providers, such as payments and website forms, may process information overseas.
  • We don’t sell your information. We don’t use analytics, advertising pixels or session recording.
  • Marketing emails and using your photos or results publicly are separate, optional choices. You can say no and still be coached.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing coaching@abheyrewired.com.

3. Health information and your consent

We only collect health information with your consent and only what is relevant to coaching you. We ask for it in two places.

  • Applying. You don’t need to share health details to apply. If you choose to tell us about an injury, condition or allergy on the application, we ask for your consent at that point.
  • After you join. When you first sign in, the app explains what it stores and asks for your consent before you log anything. You then complete a health screen so we can plan safely.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us. If we no longer have health information we need to coach you safely, we may have to pause or end coaching, and we will talk this through with you first.

Diet preferences such as halal or vegetarian can sometimes reveal religious or philosophical beliefs. We use them only to plan your meals.

4. How we use it

We use your information to respond to applications, build and adjust your training and nutrition plans, track your progress, give feedback and support, run your account and payments, keep the service secure and meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information or share it for anyone else’s marketing. We send you marketing emails only if you opt in, and every marketing email lets you opt out. Coaching messages about your plan are not marketing.

We never publish your progress photos, results or testimonials unless you give separate, specific permission. That permission is optional, does not affect your coaching and can be withdrawn at any time. Withdrawing stops future use, although material already published may not be fully retrievable.

6. Cookies, analytics and tracking

We don’t use analytics tools, advertising pixels, social media trackers or session recording on the website or in the app. The app saves your sign-in session in your browser so you stay signed in, and may save an unsent workout draft on your device for the current visit. These are needed for the app to work.

If we ever want to add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this policy first and ask for your permission where the law requires it. We would not run advertising tools on the app or on pages where you enter health information.

7. Security and how long we keep it

Every table in the app database is protected so that a client can only reach their own records and Abhey can reach his clients’ records. Photo and file storage is private, and files are served only to signed-in people with permission. Sign-in uses one-time codes sent by email. No online service is completely risk-free, so please keep your email account secure.

We keep your information while you are a client and for as long as we need it for support, disputes and legal record-keeping, then delete or de-identify it. Australian tax law generally requires business financial records to be kept for five years.

If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify the people affected and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as the law requires.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything inaccurate.
  • Ask us to delete your information where we are able to. We may need to keep some records for legal or tax reasons, and we will tell you which.
  • Withdraw consent for health information, marketing emails or use of your photos.
  • Deal with us using a pseudonym where it is practical, for example when asking a general question before you apply.

Email coaching@abheyrewired.com. We will confirm your identity before releasing or deleting records. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Questions and complaints

If you have a privacy question or complaint, email coaching@abheyrewired.com. We will look into it and reply. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

We may update this policy when our services or information practices change. The date at the top shows the current version.
