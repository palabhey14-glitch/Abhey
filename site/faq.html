FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What to expect before, during and after your coaching: getting started, payment, food, support and your privacy.

Questions, answered.

Still unsure about something? Ask me on a free 30-minute call.

Book a Strategy Call
How do I get access to my plan once I join?

Once you’ve enrolled, we start with a kickoff call. I set up your targets and your programme, and you get an email to sign in to your own Rewired app with a one-time code. Your training, meal plan, habits and check-ins are all in there, on your phone or your computer.

Is this a one-off payment or a subscription?

Premium Coaching is paid in instalments, with the amounts and dates set after our call. It’s not a subscription, and it never renews automatically.

Rewired Coaching is different. It’s an ongoing subscription, billed weekly. The details and its own FAQ are on the Pricing page.

Can I follow this with Punjabi, Indian, halal or vegetarian food?

Yes. Your plan is built around the food you grew up on and the way you already eat, whether that’s vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian or halal. For halal, you choose the products and brands you trust, and I build your meals and targets around them.

Can I still eat out and have a drink socially?

Yes. I follow an 80/20 approach: around 80% of the time you stick closely to your plan, and the other 20% leaves room for dinners out, celebrations and the odd drink. I’d rather build a programme that fits your social life responsibly than one you can’t keep up, so we plan ahead for big weekends and adjust the week around them.

What’s your refund or guarantee policy?

I back the coaching with the Week 8 Progress Promise. If you haven’t noticed a difference in your physique by the end of week 8, I keep coaching you for free. At week 8 we talk about what your coaching looks like next, and if you want to carry on, I offer you reduced-price loyalty coaching.

The promise is part of Premium Coaching. It’s a guarantee of extra coaching, not a refund, and change-of-mind refunds aren’t offered. Your rights under Australian Consumer Law still apply. The details are in the Terms & Conditions.

What if I get injured or need to pause?

Tell me as soon as it happens. Often we can train around an injury, and if you need to pause, we’ll agree the arrangement in writing. If you have to stop because of injury or illness and can give me a doctor’s certificate, I’ll refund the unused part of your programme pro rata. The first four weeks are the minimum commitment and aren’t refundable. Refunds are processed within 14 days.

I’m new to the gym. Is this good for beginners?

Yes. Your programme starts from where you are today, with exercises, weights and volume chosen for your level, and I show you exactly how to do each movement. It progresses as you get stronger and more confident.

What makes Abhey different from a regular online personal trainer?

I’ve spent more than 15 years coaching and living body recomposition myself, losing fat and building muscle at the same time, many times over. You don’t get a template and a login. You get a plan built around your life and someone who takes you through it step by step, reviewing your numbers every week and telling you exactly what to change next.

How long before I start seeing results?

In my experience, most people notice the first changes within about two weeks: more energy, better sleep, clothes fitting differently and stronger sessions. Visible changes in the mirror take longer, and everyone’s body responds at its own pace, so I track your progress every week and adjust the plan to keep it moving.

Do I get support while I’m on the programme?

Yes, and it’s personal. You log everything in the app and I review it. You can message me on My personal WhatsApp, 7 days a week, with replies within 12 hours. Each week you check in, and you get a voice note from me and fortnightly video check-ins, so you always know what to do next.

Are my photos and data private?

Yes. Your progress photos, check-ins and data are protected in your private app account, and only you and I can see them. I never publish or share your photos or results without your permission.
