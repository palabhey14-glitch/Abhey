Gym, home or hotel room. Vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, halal, or simply the food you grew up on.

Most plans fall apart by Thursday.

Not because you lack discipline. Usually the plan was written for someone with an empty calendar and a different kitchen.

So I start with your actual week: the hours you work, when you travel, and the food you grew up on, which stays on your plate. Then we set targets you can hit on a normal week, not a perfect one.

Everything is tailored to your goals and the way you live. You won’t be handed a template and left to work it out. I take you through it step by step: what to train, what to eat, what to log and what changes next.

Each week you log your meals, training and habits in the app. I review it with you and adjust your plan, so you always know exactly what to do next, from your first day to week twelve.
