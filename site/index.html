A note from me

This is a complete body recomposition. Lose the softness, build real shape, and become unrecognisable to the man who started.

If it hasn’t worked before, it’s not because you lack discipline. Usually the plan was written for someone with an empty calendar and a different kitchen.

So I start with your actual week. The hours you work, the trips you take and the food you grew up on all shape the plan, and that food stays on your plate. Then we set targets you can hit on a normal week, not a perfect one.

You won’t be handed a template and left to work it out. Every week you check in with me personally, and you can message me directly whenever you need to. It’s me on the other end, not a chatbot or an assistant.

Each week you log your meals, training and habits in the app. I review it with you and adjust your plan, so you always know exactly what to do next.
