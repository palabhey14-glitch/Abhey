How it works

What working together actually looks like.

  1. 01

    Apply or book a call

    Tell me about your goal, your week and your food. The application takes about four minutes and doesn’t commit you to anything.

    Your part. Honest answers.

  2. 02

    A free Strategy Call

    Thirty minutes with me. We go through where you are, what’s held you back and what I’d change first. If it’s a fit, I share the investment and send an enrolment form. Nothing is paid on the call.

    Your part. Turn up and ask anything.

  3. 03

    Kickoff and setup

    A kickoff call to set your targets. I build your training program tailored to your needs, plan your meals around your food and set up your personalised app.

    Your part. Give me regular updates, so I have the most data to work with and can guide you through your transformation.

  4. 04

    Your weekly rhythm

    You train, log your food, weight and steps, and check in once a week. I review it, adjust your plan and send feedback accordingly. Every fortnight we do a video check-in.

    Your part. Three or four sessions, honest logging, one check-in.

  5. 05

    Week 8, and after

    At week 8 we review your progress together and talk about what comes next. You finish with a plan to keep your results, so you don’t slide back.

    Your part. Keep showing up on the ordinary weeks. It’s all about sustainability.

Is this for you?

Honest about who this suits, and who it doesn’t.

A good fit if

  • You work long hours or travel, and you’re tired of starting over.
  • You can train three or four times a week, for 45 to 60 minutes.
  • You’re willing to log your food and check in every week.
  • You want a coach to make the decisions, so you can focus on doing them.
  • You want to keep eating the food you grew up on.

Not the right fit if

  • You’re looking for the cheapest plan available.
  • You’d rather not log food or complete weekly check-ins.
  • You want a crash diet or a big result by next month.
  • You want contest prep or a show peak. That’s a different kind of coaching.
  • You need medical treatment or a clinical diet. Coaching doesn’t replace your doctor.
  • You’re under 18. Rewired coaching is for adults only.
