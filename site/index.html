    Do I have to give up rice and roti?

    No. They're built into your macros. Portions and timing change; the food stays.

    I'm vegetarian or vegan. Can I still build muscle?

    Yes. Your protein comes from dal, chana, paneer, tofu, soy, dairy or a plant protein, planned to hit your daily target. Halal and pescatarian plans work the same way.

    I travel a lot for work.

    Every session has a hotel version, and the app works on your phone wherever you are.

    How much time does it take each day?

    About 2 minutes to log, plus 3 to 4 training sessions of 45 to 60 minutes a week.
