Gym, home or hotel room. Vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, halal, or simply the food you grew up on.

Most plans fall apart by Thursday.

Not because you lack discipline. Usually the plan was written for someone with an empty calendar and a different kitchen.

So I start with your actual week: the hours you work, when you travel, and the food you grew up on, which stays on your plate. Then we set targets you can hit on a normal week, not a perfect one.

Each week you log your meals, training and habits in the app. I go through them, and we adjust. Nothing clever. Just the basics, done properly, for twelve weeks.

Abhey

What that looks like.

  1. Training you can do anywhere

    Three or four sessions a week, 45 to 60 minutes each. Every session has a gym, home and hotel version, so a work trip doesn’t end the week.

  2. Food you already eat

    Your plan is built from your preferred cultural meals, with clear portions and protein swaps. You don’t need to cook separately from your family.

  3. Feedback from me, every week

    A voice note each week on how it actually went, a video check-in every fortnight, and WhatsApp Monday to Friday. I reply within 12 business hours.
