After you join, we ask you to complete a health screen in the app. Please tell us about injuries, health conditions, medications, allergies and any changes, so your plan stays safe and relevant. If you have a health concern, speak with a qualified health professional before starting. Stop any activity and seek appropriate care if you feel unwell or notice concerning symptoms.

Our coaching is general fitness and nutrition guidance. It does not replace medical, psychological or dietetic care, and we do not diagnose or treat conditions. If your answers suggest you need a doctor's clearance, we may ask for it before you start or before a particular part of your plan. You decide whether to perform each exercise, and accurate check-ins help us adjust your plan. We will provide our services with due care and skill.

5. The Week 8 Progress Promise (Premium Coaching)

Notice the difference by week 8. Or I keep coaching you.

If you have not noticed a difference in your physique by the end of week 8, I will keep coaching you for free. At week 8 we will talk about what your coaching beyond week 12 looks like, and if you want to carry on, I will offer you reduced-price loyalty coaching.

The Week 8 Progress Promise applies only to Premium Coaching. It is a guarantee of extra coaching, not a refund, and it does not limit any remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law. Results vary between people. The promise is about continuing support when you have not yet noticed a difference.

How to use it

We review your progress together at the end of week 8. You can also raise it with me by email or WhatsApp. If you have not noticed a difference, we agree in writing what your free coaching beyond week 12 includes and how long it runs.

6. Rewired Coaching weekly subscription

First-month commitment

Your first four weeks are a committed coaching period. Once your fourth weekly payment has been taken, you can cancel at any time.

How to cancel

Email coaching@abheyrewired.com. Cancelling stops future payments, and your coaching continues until the end of the week you have already paid for.

Eight-week satisfaction policy

If you’re not satisfied with your results after completing eight weeks of coaching, and you have followed your program at least 80% of the time, I’ll refund the coaching fees you paid for the previous four weeks. That is your last four weekly payments (AUD $320). It covers four weeks, not the full eight, and it is not a promise of a particular physical result.

To ask for it, email coaching@abheyrewired.com. I review every request against your app logs and check-ins. Set when the refund can be requested (only at the end of week 8, within a set window after it, or any time after week 8), and whether a request also cancels the subscription.

This section applies only to the weekly subscription. The Week 8 Progress Promise does not apply to the weekly subscription, and the eight-week satisfaction policy does not apply to Premium Coaching.

7. Pauses, stopping and refunds

Pauses

Tell us promptly if illness, injury or a major life event affects your coaching. Often we can train around an injury. If a pause makes sense, we will agree it with you in writing, including when coaching restarts. We will not add a new payment or extend a commitment without your agreement. Decide whether there is a maximum pause length, and whether the Week 8 timeline pauses too.

Premium Coaching

Premium Coaching does not offer change-of-mind refunds. This does not affect your rights under the Australian Consumer Law, set out in section 11.

If you have to stop because of injury or illness and give me a doctor’s certificate, I will refund the unused part of your coaching pro rata. Every client must stay for at least the first four weeks, and those weeks are not refundable. Confirm how the pro-rata amount works when not all 3 payments have been made yet.

Weekly subscription

Your first four weeks are the minimum period. After that you can cancel at any time, as set out in section 6, along with the eight-week satisfaction policy.

If we end coaching

If I find misuse or abuse, including abusive behaviour, misuse of the app or a serious breach of these terms, I may suspend or end your coaching. Whether to do so is my decision alone. If I end your coaching, I will refund the unused part of what you paid, pro rata, except that the first four weeks are locked in and are not refundable.

How refunds are paid

Agreed refunds are processed within 14 days and go back to the card you paid with.
