Our coaching is general fitness and nutrition guidance. It does not replace medical, psychological or dietetic care, and we do not diagnose or treat conditions. You remain responsible for deciding whether to perform an exercise and for providing accurate check-ins so we can adjust your plan. We will provide services with due care and skill.

5. The Week 8 Progress Promise

Notice the difference by week 8. Or the coaching is on me.

If you have not noticed a meaningful difference by the end of week 8, tell Abhey. He will continue coaching you beyond week 8 at no additional coaching cost, refining your training and nutrition with you until you are happy with your progress.

This is an additional coaching commitment, not a refund offer. It does not limit any remedy available to you under the Australian Consumer Law. Results vary between people; this promise is about continuing the support when progress has not yet felt meaningful to you.

If you want to stop coaching early, email us. We will explain what has been delivered, any agreed payments still due and any refund or other remedy available in your circumstances. Privacy policy

How we handle your information

Last updated: 22 October 2026. Rewired Coaching is run by Abhey Pal, ABN 73 322 472 368, Australia. Contact: coaching@abheyrewired.com.

What we collect

  • Applications: name, age, email, mobile, work, goals and anything else you write in the form.
  • Clients: your profile (including height, age, injuries or medical notes), daily logs (weight, steps, food, training), weekly check-ins, body measurements and progress photos. Some of this is health information, which the law treats as sensitive.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

Why we collect it

Only to assess your application and to coach you: setting your targets, tracking progress and giving you feedback. We don't sell your information or use it for anyone else's marketing. We collect health information only with your consent, which you give when you first sign in to the app.

Who can see it

You and your coach. Progress photos are kept in private storage and are never public. We won't use your photos or results in marketing unless you give separate written permission.

Where it's stored

The website is hosted by Netlify, and app data is stored by Supabase. These providers may store information on servers outside Australia. We use them because they keep data encrypted and access-controlled.

How long we keep it

For as long as you're a client and for 7 years afterwards so we can support you and meet our legal obligations, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing coaching@abheyrewired.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, contact us first; you can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Health disclaimer

Coaching is general fitness and nutrition guidance, not medical advice. Check with your GP before starting a new training or nutrition program, and tell your coach about any injury or condition.
