Each palette is shown on two example Instagram carousel slides (a cover and an inside slide) at the real 4:5 feed size. Contrast ratios use WCAG 2.x: 4.5 or higher passes for body text, 3 or higher passes for large headlines only.

A. Clear Sky

Recommended

A bright, clean blue on white and pale sky, with warm sand as the only accent. Reads as trustworthy without looking like a big bank, and the sand CTA gives paid posts something to stop the scroll.

B. Coastal

Softer

A muted ocean blue with misty aqua and a coral accent. Warmer and more lifestyle-led, which suits first home buyers, but coral is a common choice for property and lifestyle accounts right now, so it stands out less.

C. Harbour

Brightest

Deep navy carries the text and a lighter cornflower adds brightness, with butter yellow as the accent. The most playful of the three. Cornflower is too light for small text, so it only works for shapes and big headlines.
