# Brand Color Palettes

Working shortlist for the brand. Pick **one primary, one secondary, one accent**
and lock the hex codes into Canva (Brand Kit → Colors).

> Options 1–14 have not been added yet. The numbers below (15–20) are inferred
> from the notes at the bottom, which reference options 16, 18, 19 and 20.
> Confirm the numbering before anyone uses it in a brief.

Contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x. **AA** needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for
large text (24px+, or 18.5px+ bold). Anything under 3:1 is decoration only,
never text.

## Culturally Tailored

### 16. Deep Red and Gold Foil

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Deep red | `#8B1E2B` |
| Accent | Gold | `#D4AF37` |
| Text | Black | `#000000` |

| Pairing | Ratio | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| White on deep red | 9.06 | AA body text |
| Black on gold | 9.99 | AA body text |
| Gold on deep red | 4.31 | Large text / headlines only |
| **Black on deep red** | **2.32** | **Fails — do not use** |

Fix: black text only goes on gold or light backgrounds. On deep red, use white
or cream text. Flat `#D4AF37` will not look like foil on screen; the "foil"
effect needs a gradient or texture in Canva.
